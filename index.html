SingIt — AI Karaoke

Sing any song. Get scored. Share your voice.
TOP PICK — Vannes's Idea

The Problem: Traditional karaoke apps have limited song libraries because of licensing. Users can only sing pre-approved tracks. Subscriptions are expensive and the UX feels stuck in 2015.

The Solution: AI vocal removal lets users turn ANY song into karaoke — import from your library, YouTube, or Spotify. AI scores pitch accuracy, timing, and tone in real-time. Record, share, challenge friends.

Why This Wins: "Sing ANY song" is the killer feature. No other karaoke app does this well. The $5B+ karaoke market is dominated by apps with outdated UX and limited libraries. AI vocal separation (Demucs/Spleeter) makes this technically feasible now.

DubMe — AI Voice & Accent Transformer

Hear yourself speak any language. Sound like anyone.
HIGH PRIORITY

The Portfolio Thesis

Why multiple apps, not one big bet

Same Stack

Every app uses Expo + Claude API + Stripe. One team builds all of them. No new tech to learn.

Same Pattern

AI input → scoring/feedback → social sharing → freemium conversion. Proven loop across all ideas.

Cross-Promotion

MatchBD & Kindred users see SingIt. SingIt users see DubMe. Screen Savvy promotes MimicMe. Munchies clients get FeatCoach integration. Each app feeds the others.

Risk Diversification

If one app doesn't take off, others carry the portfolio. Consumer apps are unpredictable — volume hedges the bet.
